Support

For Claro app help, subscription questions, or privacy requests, email yanayh90@gmail.com.

Describe what happened and, if available, your app version, Android version, and device model. Share only information needed to explain the issue. Do not send PINs, recovery values, card details, private messages, contact lists, or purchase tokens.

Privacy requests

Use the same email for access, deletion, or other privacy questions. Reasonable verification may be required; historical records may not always be identifiable. Optional usage analytics, diagnostics, and campaign measurement choices are separate and off by default. Change them in Membership & privacy. See the privacy policy for details and limits.

Subscriptions and refunds

Manage or cancel subscriptions through Google Play. Uninstalling or resetting Claro, or turning off optional data collection, does not cancel a subscription. For refunds, see Google Play refund information; applicable mandatory consumer rights still apply.

Urgent help

This email inbox and Claro are not emergency services. Support is not monitored as an emergency channel and no response time is guaranteed. For an emergency, contact local emergency services directly. App call/message submission or SOS/contact handoff is not a guarantee of connection, delivery, or assistance.
