Privacy policy

Effective and last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy explains how the Claro Android app and this public information site handle information. For privacy questions or requests, contact the Claro app publisher at yanayh90@gmail.com.

App information and permissions

Claro does not require an app account or registration. For actions you request, the app may access Android contacts, calling and call logs, SMS/MMS, selected or shared photos and media, and installed-app launch functionality. Access depends on the relevant Android permissions and default-app roles. You can manage permissions in Android settings; restricting access may prevent the related action.

Android, mobile carriers, destination apps you choose, and emergency services may process information when you use their services. Their handling of that information is separate from Claro's optional measurement choices.

Names, telephone numbers, message contents, contacts, photos, selected-app identity, and PIN or recovery values are not deliberately sent in application analytics. This is not a promise that every automatically captured fatal diagnostic report is free of sensitive information: operating-system or library details can appear in fatal crash and application-not-responding reports. Handled error reports are scrubbed.

Optional collection: separate choices, off by default

Usage analytics, diagnostics, and advertising/campaign measurement are independent saved choices, each off by default. Where a service is configured and enabled for the release, collection requires its relevant consent. You can change these choices in Membership & privacy.

  • Usage analytics (Mixpanel and Google Firebase Analytics): helps understand app use through coarse screen, setup and readiness outcomes, app sessions, visual and text-size settings, generic feature outcomes, and verified purchase conversions. Firebase may also generate its own automatic events after analytics consent. Mixpanel automatic event collection, autocapture, and IP-based geolocation enrichment are disabled.
  • Diagnostics (Firebase Crashlytics): helps investigate crashes and reliability problems. Automatic uploads are disabled. With saved crash consent from a prior session, reports may be explicitly uploaded at the next startup. SDKs may retain diagnostics locally.
  • Advertising/campaign measurement (Meta App Events): is not enabled for release pending privacy and network review. If enabled in a future release with the relevant consent, it is limited to app activation/install, setup completion, membership views, checkout, and trial, subscription, and charged-purchase conversions. It does not receive call, SMS or SOS events, visual/text-size settings, or readiness data.

Claro stores a random installation identifier and shares the same identifier with Mixpanel and Firebase Analytics when usage analytics is enabled, and with Firebase Crashlytics when diagnostics is enabled. This lets records from the same installation be associated across those services only under their respective consent choices. The identifier is not based on your name, email, telephone number, or a hardware identifier. It is not a person or account ID and does not identify you across devices or reinstalls. Meta and RevenueCat use separate identities.

When both usage analytics and diagnostics are off, Claro removes the saved installation identifier as part of saving those choices. Enabling either choice again generates a fresh identifier. This does not erase previously sent information.

These providers may process technical app, device, installation, and network identifiers and IP addresses; optional collection is not necessarily anonymous. Advertising ID collection is disabled, and the app does not request advertising ID or Android AdServices permissions. Disabling IP geolocation enrichment does not prevent a provider from processing network information when receiving a request.

Verified conversion measurement may include a coarse product tier, trial/subscription status, and charged amount and currency. Raw order IDs and purchase tokens are not passed into analytics.

Turning a choice off stops new submissions made by Claro for that choice. It does not delete information already sent. Previously queued or in-flight uploads may not be recallable; Meta queue erasure or complete SDK shutdown cannot be guaranteed.

Purchases and subscriptions

Google Play and RevenueCat, when configured, process purchases, entitlement verification, and purchase restoration separately from optional analytics consent. Google Play processes payment details; the app publisher does not receive card details through this integration. RevenueCat may process SDK-generated app-user identifiers, purchase and transaction identifiers, entitlement status, and technical information. This processing is distinct from optional conversion measurement.

Storage, reset, and retention

App preferences, configuration, drafts, and related settings may be stored on your device. Claro does not promise an app-operated cloud backup or sync service. Local reset clears the app-owned information described by the reset flow; it does not bulk-delete Android contacts, SMS, or shared photos, cancel subscriptions, or guarantee deletion of data already transmitted to a provider.

Retention depends on the information, its purpose, provider settings and policies, and applicable requirements. We do not state a fixed retention period for every provider. Device reset, app reset, uninstalling, or withdrawing optional consent does not itself erase previously transmitted provider data.

Providers and international processing

The providers below may process information in countries other than yours, under their applicable terms and privacy practices. Their policies explain their processing and retention in more detail:

  • Google privacy policy and Firebase privacy information (Android-related services, Google Play, analytics, and diagnostics).
  • Mixpanel privacy policy (optional usage analytics).
  • Meta privacy policy (campaign measurement, if enabled).
  • RevenueCat privacy policy (purchase and entitlement services).
  • GitHub privacy statement (hosting this site through GitHub Pages).

This website and support email

This site does not add analytics, tracking pixels, cookies, external fonts, or scripts. GitHub Pages receives ordinary web-request information, including IP addresses, to serve the site and for security. If you email support, the app publisher and email providers process the address and information you send to respond to your request. Please do not send PINs, recovery values, payment details, private messages, or contact lists.

Your requests and security

Contact yanayh90@gmail.com for access, deletion, or other privacy questions. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing, receive a copy of data, withdraw consent, or contact a privacy authority. Reasonable verification may be needed. We may not be able to locate historical records that are not linked to information you can provide, and legal requirements can affect what can be deleted.

Security measures cannot guarantee absolute protection. Changes to this policy will be published here with an updated date; any newly required consent must be obtained separately.
